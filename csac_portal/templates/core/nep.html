NEP 2020 Implementation

In alignment with the National Education Policy (NEP) 2020, Chaitanya Science and Arts College has adopted the new educational framework to transition towards a more holistic, flexible, and multidisciplinary system of higher education. NEP 2020 aims to empower students by giving them choice and flexibility, focusing on skill development, and fostering research at all levels.

NEP UG Program Structure

Exit Point Duration Credential Awarded Min. Credits Required
Exit after Year 1 2 Semesters UG Certificate 40 - 44 Credits

NEP Student Checklist & Action Plan
  1. Register on the ABC (Academic Bank of Credits) portal to get your unique ABC ID.
  2. Choose your Major and Minor courses during admission counseling.
  3. Select your Multi-Disciplinary Course (MDC) and Skill Enhancement Course (SEC) based on interest.
  4. Participate in Internship / Apprenticeship / Community Service during vacations.
