Internal Quality Assurance Cell (IQAC)

In pursuance of its Action Plan for performance evaluation, assessment, accreditation, and quality up-gradation of institutions of higher education, the college established the Internal Quality Assurance Cell (IQAC). The primary aim of IQAC is to develop a system for conscious, consistent, and catalytic action to improve the academic and administrative performance of Chaitanya Science and Arts College.
