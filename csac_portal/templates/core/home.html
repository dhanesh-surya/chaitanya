About Us

Chaitanya Science and Arts College, established in 2001 in Pamgarh, Chhattisgarh, offers multidisciplinary undergraduate and postgraduate programs in sciences, humanities, commerce, and management to educate rural students. The college focuses on providing quality education and a conducive learning environment while developing critical thinking and leadership skills. The campus features modern infrastructure, including playgrounds, gardens, and a modern auditorium.
